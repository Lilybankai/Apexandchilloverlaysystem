What never leaves your PC

  • Your voice. Push-to-talk audio for the race engineer is transcribed on your own PC and the recordings are deleted; audio is never uploaded.
  • Streaming logins. If you link Twitch or YouTube, the tokens are stored only on your machine and talk directly to Twitch and Google — they are never sent to our servers.
  • Your full lap history. Every lap you drive is recorded to local files on your PC. Only the boards' best laps, daily totals, and the per-lap fuel/tyre and pit-stop readings described above are uploaded.
  • Viewer chat messages pass through the app live and are not stored.

Who sees what

  • Other signed-in users can see your display name, your leaderboard laps and their driving traces, and the setups you publish with their ratings. That is the point of those features — don't put anything in a display name, setup name or note you wouldn't share.
  • League admins can additionally see your email address, how often you use the app, your feedback, and your subscription status, to run the league and support the app.
  • The race-engineer summary can include the display names of the other drivers in your session (so the answer can name your rival).

Retention

Everything tied to your account is kept while the account exists and deleted when it is deleted. Engineer question logs are kept to improve the feature and are deleted with your account. Marketing emails are only sent if you opted in, and you can opt out any time.

Deleting your account — your right to erasure

Settings → Account & privacy → Delete account & data permanently deletes your account: your profile, laps, driving traces, fuel and pit-stop records, published setups, ratings, downloads, engineer questions, usage records, feedback and billing record are erased, and any live subscription is cancelled. This is immediate and cannot be undone. Files on your own PC (your local lap history and settings) stay yours — uninstalling the app or deleting its folders removes them.
