## Message 2 — what lands on your screen

## 📊 On track

Every widget works two ways at once: an **OBS Browser Source** for the stream, and an **in-game layer** over the sim. Drag them where you want, resize them, and fade the panel backgrounds from solid to invisible — per widget, live, no reload, no re-copied URL.

▸ **Standings** — full field, gaps, pit status, average-pace column
▸ **Relative** — nearest cars, live delta, each tagged with its class (HY / P2 / P3 / GT3 / GTE / GT4) in that class's colour, so an arriving car reads as a rival or as traffic before you've read the gap
▸ **Speedo cluster** — the whole panel lights from the floor up as the revs rise, green through amber to red, so you shift without looking at a number. Fuel, virtual energy, projected lap and the hybrid battery around it — the battery glows green whenever regen is feeding charge back in
▸ **Radar** — a spotter's strip of the cars around you, drawn to scale, plus the pit-release light
▸ **Track map** — the circuit as a raised 2.5-D ribbon with every car on it, learned from your own first lap there
▸ **Track limits** — how much of your points allowance is left, what each cut cost you, and the sim's own penalties
▸ **Fuel calculator** — per-lap use, laps remaining, fuel to finish, pit window
▸ **Pedals** (trace or vertical), **Motion** (G-meter / rotation / attitude), **Tyre temps**, **Weather forecast**
▸ **Damage & Repair** — what's broken and a live countdown once the crew is on the car *(LMU)*
▸ **MFD** — a read-only mirror of the in-game pit menu and driving aids *(LMU)*

## Message 3 — the race engineer

## 🎙️ A race engineer on the radio

Bind a wheel button, press it, wait for the chirp, ask.

▸ **28 questions answered instantly — on your PC, offline, unlimited.** Gaps, who's ahead and what they're doing, backmarkers, fuel and energy to the flag, tyres, damage and repair time, brake bias, track-limit points, yellows, weather. Phrases work inside a sentence: "mate, what's the gap ahead right now" lands the same as "gap ahead"

▸ **He calls the race.** Green flag, chequered, final lap, safety car, red flag, penalties given and served, damage, the fuel window. Turn the dial up for the race story on top — your fastest laps, the field's, places gained and lost, rivals pitting, blue flags — or down to answer-only

▸ **And he shuts up when he should.** Never while you're side by side or deep in the brakes, never reads out what's already on your screen, drops a call rather than delivering it late, and your question always cuts in front of his

▸ **Advanced questions.** Ask the thing the phrase list can't cover — "should I pit under this safety car?" — and he works it out from your live race. 300 a month; the instant answers stay unlimited

Six voices, through a proper comms channel — band-limited, squelch, hiss under the voice. Your mic is only live for the few seconds after you press the button. Never in the background.

## Message 4 — everything off track

## 🔧 Off track

▸ **Setup editor** *(LMU)* — ten intent sliders (front turn-in, rear traction, top speed, softer over kerbs, braking stability and friends), each moving a weighted set of real settings at once. Nothing touches the car while you drag — rows preview old → new and one **Apply** sends the lot

▸ **A setup library** — save the car as the garage holds it, tag it Race or Quali, and sort it by *your best clean lap* on that track, pulled live from your lap database. Share a tune as a file, or copy it straight into a Discord message

▸ **Community setups** — filtered to the exact car and track you're sitting in, and it re-filters itself when you swap

▸ **Fuel & strategy planner** — the whole race: total fuel or Virtual Energy, how many stops, every stint's fill and lap count, and what each stop costs under LMU's refuel-then-tyres rule. Plus the strategies that would save a stop — and an honest word when that consumption target is a fantasy

▸ **League leaderboards** — every member's best clean lap, by track and class, your own row marked

▸ **Reference pace** — your best lap as a percentage of alien pace for your class at your layout, on the Alien → Offline ladder. Answers "am I actually quick here?" with nobody else on track. *(Reference times are Ohne Speed's work, credited in the app.)*

▸ **Schedule** — our Thursday (LMP2 & GT3) and Saturday (GT3) championships pulled live from SimGrid: next race, rounds left, and a sign-up button

▸ **Streamers tab** — YouTube and Twitch chat as one overlay column, plus a StreamBot that speaks as *your own account*: !commands, timed messages, sub / member / Super Chat alerts and goals. No third-party bot to invite, no cloud relay, your logins stay on your PC

## Message 6 — getting started

## ⚙️ Up and running in about five minutes

**1.** Install it → https://github.com/Lilybankai/Apexandchilloverlaysystem/releases/download/v0.90.3/Apex-AIO-System-Setup-0.90.3.exe
*(Windows. It's a chunky download — the race engineer's voice ships inside the installer so there's nothing to fetch later.)*

**2.** Create your account — or hit **Continue offline** if you only want the overlays on your own PC.

**3.** Redeem your league code, or start the trial.

**4.** Work down the **Get started checklist** on the Dashboard. It covers the telemetry plugin, giving the sim's own controls a key, binding your buttons, getting an overlay on screen, laying the widgets out and setting up the engineer — and each row ticks itself when the thing is genuinely done, so it tells you what's left rather than asking. Click a row and it runs a guided tour of that section.

**5. For OBS:** Overlays tab → **Copy** on any widget → paste into a Browser Source.
**5. For in game:** flip **Show in game**, then **Edit layout** and drag things where you want them. Widgets snap flush to each other and match their neighbour's size, so a row of panels comes out aligned.

Updates arrive in the app itself — it offers them, never installs one out from under you mid-stream.

**Bug, or something you want added?** The **Suggestions** tab sends it straight to me with your version attached, and you get told when it's answered. Or just shout in #suggestions.
