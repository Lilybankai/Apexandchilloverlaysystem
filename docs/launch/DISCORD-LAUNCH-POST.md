## Message 2 — what lands on your screen

## 📊 On track

Every widget works two ways at once: an **OBS Browser Source** for the stream, and an **in-game layer** over the sim. Drag them where you want, resize them, and fade the panel backgrounds from solid to invisible — per widget, live, no reload.

▸ **Standings** — full field, gaps, pit status, average-pace column
▸ **Relative** — nearest cars, live delta, each tagged with its class (HY / P2 / P3 / GT3 / GTE / GT4) in that class's colour, so an arriving car reads as a rival or as traffic before you've read the gap
▸ **Speedo cluster** — the whole panel lights from the floor up as the revs rise, green through amber to red, so you shift without looking at a number. Fuel, virtual energy, projected lap and the hybrid battery around it
▸ **Ten LMGT3 dashboards, plus the Apex Real cluster** — a design drawn from the real car's own display for the Porsche, Aston, BMW, Corvette, Ferrari, Mustang, Huracán, Lexus, McLaren and AMG. Pick yours from the Design dropdown
▸ **Radar** — a spotter's strip of the cars around you, to scale, plus the pit-release light
▸ **Track map** — the circuit as a raised 2.5-D ribbon with every car on it, learned from your own first lap there
▸ **Track limits** — points allowance left, what each cut cost, and the sim's own penalties
▸ **Fuel calculator** — per-lap use, laps remaining, fuel to finish, pit window
▸ **Pedals**, **Motion** (G / rotation / attitude), **Tyre temps**, **Weather** — and °C or °F, your choice
▸ **Damage & Repair** — what's broken, live repair countdown *(LMU)*
▸ **MFD** — read-only mirror of the pit menu and aids, upright or on its side *(LMU)*

## Message 3 — the race engineer

## 🎙️ A race engineer on the radio

Bind a wheel button, press it, wait for the chirp, ask.

▸ **37 questions answered instantly — on your PC, offline, unlimited.** Gaps, who's ahead and what they're doing, backmarkers, fuel and energy to the flag, tyres, damage and repair time, brake bias, track-limit points, yellows, weather. Around 126 ways of asking, and phrases work inside a sentence: "mate, what's the gap ahead right now" lands the same as "gap ahead"

▸ **One-word calls work.** "Tyres." "Temps." "Fuel." The short ones you actually make mid-corner. They used to die in Windows' own 2006-era recogniser; he now hears everything through the good one

▸ **He calls the race.** Green flag, chequered, final lap, safety car, red flag, penalties given and served, damage, the fuel window. Turn the dial up for the race story on top — your fastest laps, the field's, places gained and lost, rivals pitting, blue flags — or down to answer-only

▸ **He calls yellows by sector.** "Yellow flag, sector two — watch for a slow car through there", and the all-clear when it withdraws. Read live from the sim's shared memory, so it's the real sector rather than a guess

▸ **And he coaches.** Ask for **"target pace"** and he reads your best lap against the reference bands and tells you what the next band up actually costs you

▸ **He shuts up when he should.** Never while you're side by side or deep in the brakes, never reads out what's already on your screen, and your question always cuts in front of his

▸ **Advanced questions** — "should I pit under this safety car?" — worked out from your live race. 300 a month; the instant answers stay unlimited

▸ **Loud rig?** An optional **Better ears** model on the Engineer tab — one 466 MB download, and every question is heard by the sharper model. Still all on your PC

Six voices, through a proper comms channel — band-limited, squelch, hiss. Your mic is only live for the few seconds after you press the button. Never in the background.

## Message 4 — everything off track

## 🔧 Off track

▸ **Setup editor** *(LMU)* — ten intent sliders (front turn-in, rear traction, top speed, softer over kerbs, braking stability and friends), each moving a weighted set of real settings at once. Nothing touches the car while you drag — rows preview old → new and one **Apply** sends the lot

▸ **A setup library** — save the car as the garage holds it, tag it Race or Quali, and sort it by *your best clean lap* on that track. Share a tune as a file, or copy it straight into a Discord message

▸ **Community setups** — filtered to the exact car and track you're sitting in, and it re-filters itself when you swap

▸ **League leaderboards** — every member's best clean lap, by track and class, your own row marked

▸ **Reference pace** — your best lap as a percentage of alien pace for your class at your layout, on the Alien → Offline ladder. Answers "am I actually quick here?" with nobody else on track. *(Reference times are Ohne Speed's work, credited in the app.)*

▸ **Schedule** — our Thursday (LMP2 & GT3) and Saturday (GT3) championships pulled live from SimGrid: next race, rounds left, and a sign-up button

▸ **Streamers** — YouTube and Twitch chat as one overlay column, plus a StreamBot that speaks as *your own account*: !commands, timed messages, sub / member / Super Chat alerts and goals. No third-party bot, no cloud relay, your logins stay on your PC
